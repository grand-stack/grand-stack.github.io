This page explains what the GRANDstack website (www.grandstack.ai) collects about visitors. The site is operated by Neo4j (see the copyright notice in each repository).

## The short version

- The site works fully **without** any tracking. If you do nothing, or press **Decline**, nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is stored about you for analytics.
- If you press **Accept**, the site uses analytics from **Mixpanel** (EU data centre) to count visits and to see how visitors move through the site, in particular how many follow links to GitHub.
- We respect your browser's **Global Privacy Control** and **Do Not Track** signals: with either on, analytics stays off and you are not asked.

## What is collected, if you accept

- An **anonymous random identifier**, kept in your browser's local storage (not shared with other sites).
- The pages you view, the site you came from (the referring website, and any `utm_` campaign parameters in the link), and your screen class (phone or desktop) and light or dark theme.
- Actions on the site: clicks on links to **GitHub** (which repository, and where on the page the link was), copying a command, choosing a setup tab or a filter, switching the theme, moving between documentation pages, how far down a page you scroll, and how long you spend on a page.
- Your **IP address**, which Mixpanel uses to work out an approximate location (country, region, city). It is not used to identify you.
- Not collected: your name, email address or anything you type, and no advertising profile is built.

## Why

To understand which parts of GRANDstack are used, which pages lead people to the code on GitHub, and where the documentation needs work.

## Where it goes

Events are sent to Mixpanel's **EU** endpoint (`api-eu.mixpanel.com`). Mixpanel processes them for us as a service provider. How long Mixpanel keeps them is set in the project's retention settings.

## Your choices

- Use **Cookie settings** in the footer at any time to accept or decline, and to change your mind. Declining after accepting clears the identifier from your browser.
- Your choice is remembered for 12 months, then you are asked again.
- To ask about your data, or for deletion, contact us by opening an issue at https://github.com/grand-stack/grand-stack.github.io/issues (do not include personal details) or through Neo4j's usual privacy contact.

## Everything else

The site is static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep its own server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement. Links to GitHub, Wikipedia and other sites take you to places with their own privacy practices.
